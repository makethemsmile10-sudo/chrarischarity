About us

A support system for those who have no one

Charis Charity Organization was founded on a simple conviction — that every elderly person and widow deserves dignity, care, and community, regardless of their circumstances.

Operating across Nigeria and the United Kingdom, we run regular outreach programmes delivering food, medical support, and essential supplies to vulnerable individuals in underserved communities.

Our mission
"To empower widows and improve the lives of the elderly in Nigeria"

Humanitarian Services

Delivering essential goods, food, and care to those most in need across Kogi State and beyond.

Medical Outreach

Partnering with hospitals to provide healthcare access for elderly patients who cannot afford treatment.

Community Empowerment

Building long-term support networks for widows and the elderly to foster independence and dignity.
